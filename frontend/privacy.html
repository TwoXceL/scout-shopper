Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 18, 2026 • Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Adonis Apps & Studios Inc. ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use Scout $hopper (scoutshopper.app), in compliance with the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and international privacy standards.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hash, referral codes, and country/province.
  • Camera & Barcode Data: When using the in-aisle scanner, camera access is used strictly in real-time on your device to decode UPC/EAN barcodes. We do not store or transmit raw camera video feeds.
  • Receipt & Purchase Data: If you use our price watchdog by forwarding e-receipts or uploading receipts, we parse merchant names, order numbers, items purchased, prices paid, and purchase dates to monitor 30-day price drops.
  • Payment Information: Payment data for digital subscriptions or merchant advertising campaigns is collected and processed directly by our Merchant of Record, Paddle. We never store your full credit card number on our servers.
  • Device & Usage Telemetry: If you opt in, we collect anonymized scan frequency, regional store availability, and app performance data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide real-time price comparisons and "Shop Small" alternatives.
  • Continuously monitor purchase prices for post-purchase refund opportunities.
  • Calculate and deliver referral bounties and gift card payouts via Tremendous API.
  • Comply with tax, legal, and regulatory requirements in Canada.

3. How We Share Information

Zero Data Brokering: We do not sell your personal identifying information to data brokers. We share data only with trusted service providers essential to app operation.
  • Paddle.com: Merchant of Record and payment processor for digital subscriptions and merchant services.
  • Tremendous: Payout and gift card disbursement engine for user referral cash-outs and milestone bonuses.
  • Cloudflare: CDN caching, DDoS mitigation, web security, and inbound receipt email routing.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as your account is active.

  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated receipt/scan data at any time through the in-app settings or by emailing privacy@scoutshopper.app.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions or concerns about our privacy practices:

  • Privacy Officer: Adonis Apps & Studios Inc.
  • Email: privacy@scoutshopper.app
  • Location: Red Deer, Alberta, Canada
